---
title: OLTP, OLAP
---

**OLTP** (Online Transactional Processing) and **OLAP** (Online Analytical Processing) are categories classified by the characteristics of Data Processing. This post analyzes OLTP and OLAP.

## 1. OLTP (Online Transaction Processing)

OLTP, as the meaning of Transaction Processing implies, refers to a Data Processing process centered on Transaction handling such as Insert, Update, and Delete of Data. It has the characteristic that a small amount of Data is changed concurrently by many Users. Processes such as Online Banking, Online book purchases, and Online accommodation reservations are classified as OLTP.

## 2. OLAP (Online Analytical Processing)

OLAP, as the meaning of Analytical Processing implies, refers to a Data Processing process centered on Data analysis. It has the characteristic that a large amount of Data is read concurrently by a small number of Users. The process of transforming Data into valuable information through Data analysis is classified as OLAP.
